# Pokemon artwork

The 4 wallpapers are art inspired by Pokemon, shared here as fan content for
personal desktops, free of charge. Each picture belongs to its artist, and the work it
shows belongs to Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures and The Pokemon Company. This theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.

- `01-pichu.jpg`: Pichu at the game desk; artist unknown
- `02-ash-and-charizard.jpg`: Ash and Charizard; artist unknown
- `03-ghost-pokemon.jpg`: Dusknoir, Mismagius and the ghost Pokemon; artist unknown
- `04-pikachu.jpg`: Pikachu; artist unknown

If one of these is yours, open an issue to be credited by name or to have it removed.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: night indigo background, cream text, Pikachu yellow accent, Poke Ball red, grass green, thunder yellow, water blue, ghost violet and ice teal.
